Itty Bitty Angler – Privacy Policy

Effective November 15, 2026

Itty Bitty Angler (“the game”) is developed by Gary Gallagher (yoGee studios). This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have.

Information stored on your device

Your progress, catches, journal, gear, coins, pearls, outfits, settings and purchase status are saved only on your device. We do not operate servers, do not ask you to create an account, and do not sell or share this information. Deleting the game deletes this data.

The game uses your device’s clock and time zone so day, night and seasons on the water match your own. This stays on your device.

Analytics and crash reports (Unity)

The game uses Unity Analytics and Unity crash reporting (Unity Technologies) to understand how the game is played and to fix problems: for example when a session starts and ends, tutorial steps finished, maps visited, fish caught, purchases completed, and crash or error reports. These services may collect a random installation identifier, device model, operating-system version, language, approximate location derived from your IP address, and game events. This information is not used to identify you or to track you across other companies’ apps and websites.

Advertising (Unity LevelPlay)

The game may offer optional reward videos, provided through Unity LevelPlay (Unity Technologies) and its ad partners. Ads are only shown when you choose to watch one for a reward; the game never interrupts play with an ad. To show and measure ads, these services may collect your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA, only if you allow tracking), IP address and approximate location, device and operating-system information, and how you interact with ads.

iOS may ask whether the game may track you. If you allow it, ads may be personalised. If you decline, reward videos still work but are not personalised using your advertising identifier. You can change this at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Unity’s privacy policy: unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

In-app purchases and subscriptions

All purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple. We never see or store your payment details. Apple tells the game only that a purchase was completed, so it can unlock what you bought. You can manage or cancel a subscription in Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions.

Sharing

If you share a catch or your Trophy Lodge, the game opens the standard iOS share sheet with an image and a line of text. Nothing is sent unless you choose where to share it.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices and rights

You can limit ad tracking in your device settings at any time, and you can delete all game data by deleting the game. For questions or requests about your data, including deletion of analytics data, contact us at the address below.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
