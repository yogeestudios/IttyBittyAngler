How to play

Steer your boat across the water and look for fish shadows under the surface. Tap the water to cast in front of a shadow and wait for the bite. When a fish is hooked, hold to reel and keep the needle in the green zone; let go when it runs hard, or the line snaps. Bigger, rarer fish fight harder. If your line snags or a fight goes wrong, you can cut the line – you lose the bait or lure on it.

Day, night and seasons

The game follows your device’s local time and date, so it is night on the water when it is night where you are, and fish behave by season.
